A Vedic chart places the twelve **bhavas (houses)** over the twelve rashis starting from
the **Lagna** — the sidereal sign rising on the eastern horizon at your birth moment.
Vyotish uses the classical **whole-sign house system**: the Lagna's sign is house 1,
and each following sign is the next house. The Lagna anchors everything; two people
born hours apart can share a Moon sign yet differ completely by rising sign.

## What each house governs

1. **Tanu (self)** — body, vitality, character, the head of the chart.
2. **Dhana (wealth)** — earnings, family, speech, accumulated assets.
3. **Sahaja (courage)** — siblings, communication, short journeys, effort.
4. **Sukha (home)** — mother, property, education, inner peace, conveyances.
5. **Putra (children)** — progeny, intellect, creativity, past-life merit.
6. **Shatru (enemies)** — illness, debts, competition, service; also daily work.
7. **Kalatra (partnership)** — spouse, marriage, business partners, the "other".
8. **Ayu (longevity)** — longevity, transformation, inheritance, the occult.
9. **Dharma (fortune)** — father, guru, dharma, long journeys, grace.
10. **Karma (career)** — profession, status, public action, authority.
11. **Labha (gains)** — income streams, networks, elder siblings, fulfilment of desire.
12. **Moksha (liberation)** — loss, foreign lands, retreat, liberation, sleep.

## Angular, succedent, cadent

Houses 1, 4, 7, 10 are the **kendras (angles)** — pillars of visible life. Houses
2, 5, 8, 11 follow as **panaparas**; 3, 6, 9, 12 as **apoklimas**. The trikona houses
1, 5, 9 carry fortune; the 6, 8, 12 trio (dusthanas) brings struggle but also
growth through service, transformation and release.

## House = sign + lord + occupants

A bhava is never read in isolation: its **sign**, the **condition of its lord**
(where that lord sits and how strong it is), and the **grahas placed in it** together
produce the verdict. Manglik analysis, for example, checks Mars in houses 1, 2, 4, 7,
8 and 12 from the Lagna — and also from the Moon and Venus — with classical
cancellations ([Mangal Dosha tool](/calculators/mangal-dosha)).
