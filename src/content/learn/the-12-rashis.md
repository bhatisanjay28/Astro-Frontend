In Vedic astrology the zodiac is **sidereal** — anchored to the fixed stars via the Lahiri
ayanamsha — and the sign the **Moon** occupied at birth is your **Rashi**, the primary
reference for rashiphal (predictions), matching and most remedial advice. The Western
"sun sign" plays a smaller role in Jyotish.

## The twelve signs at a glance

| 3   | Mithuna (Gemini)     | Mercury | Air     | Dual    |

## What the classification means

- **Element** colours the temperament: fire signs initiate with energy, earth signs
  consolidate with patience, air signs connect through ideas, water signs feel and adapt.
- **Movable / fixed / dual** describes how a sign spends its energy — starting, holding,
  or mediating. Charts with many movable signs respond fast to change; fixed signs
  persevere; dual signs negotiate.
- **The lord** of your Rashi becomes a lifelong anchor planet — its strength and
  placement (computed on the [Free Kundli](/kundli) page) says much about how the
  mind processes experience.

## Moon sign vs Sun sign

The Moon governs _manas_ — the mind, emotions, and everyday fortune. That is why
Vedic dasha periods start from the Moon's nakshatra and why rashiphal is Moon-sign
based. Your Sun sign still exists (it appears in the kundli like any other graha), but
it describes outer vitality and recognition rather than the felt life.
