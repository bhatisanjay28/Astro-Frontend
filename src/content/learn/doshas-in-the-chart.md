**Dosha** literally means "fault" or "imbalance" — a configuration in a chart that
traditionally requires attention. A dosha is a starting point for analysis, never a
verdict; every classical dosha carries known cancellation rules, and Vyotish computes
the mathematics honestly (with verification status) so that no tool of ours ever sells
fear.

## Manglik dosha (Mangal dosha)

Mars in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8 or 12 — counted from the **Lagna, the Moon and Venus** —
marks the classical Manglik check. Cancellations are equally classical: Mars in its
own signs (Aries, Scorpio) or exaltation (Capricorn) nullifies the dosha, and a
Manglik–Manglik pairing is traditionally considered mutually cancelled. The
[Mangal Dosha tool](/calculators/mangal-dosha) evaluates all three reference points
and states the cancellation reason when it applies.

## Sade Sati

Saturn's transit of the 12th, 1st and 2nd signs from the natal Moon lasts roughly
**7.5 years** in three phases. Phase boundaries and windows are computed from
Saturn's actual sidereal ingress — for an Aquarius Moon, the current window runs
2020–2028. Sade Sati is a period of consolidation and reordering, not a curse;
classical guidance favours discipline, service and steady effort through it.
Check your phase on the [Sade Sati tool](/calculators/sade-sati).

## Kaal Sarp yoga

When all seven grahas are hemmed within one Rahu–Ketu axis, the chart carries Kaal
Sarp yoga — twelve named variants by Rahu's house (Anant, Kulit, Vasuki … Sheshnag).
Its intensity and even its dosha-status is debated between schools; Vyotish reports
the configuration and its axis without dramatising it
([Kaal Sarp tool](/calculators/kaal-sarp)).

## Reading any dosha without panic

1. **Look for the cancellation** first — most doshas have them.
2. **Weigh the whole chart** — a strong Jupiter, benefic Lagna lord, or supportive
   dasha changes outcomes far more than a single configuration.
3. **Prefer action to fear** — dosha prescriptions are remedial and optional; nothing
   in Jyotish mandates anxiety or expensive "fixes".

That is Vyotish's editorial line across every calculator: show the mathematics, cite
the classical rule, flag what awaits Jyotishi review, and never monetise fear.
