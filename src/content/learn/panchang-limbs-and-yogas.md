**Panchang** means "five limbs" — the five classical attributes that define any Vedic
day: **Tithi** (lunar day), **Vaar** (weekday), **Nakshatra** (the Moon's mansion),
**Yoga** (Sun–Moon combination) and **Karana** (half-tithi). Choosing an auspicious
moment means choosing a moment whose limbs favour the activity — the foundation of
every [muhurat](/muhurat).

## The five limbs

- **Tithi** — the Moon–Sun elongation divided into 30 lunar days (12° each). The
  15 Shukla (waxing) tithis run from Pratipada to Purnima; the 15 Krishna (waning)
  from Pratipada to Amavasya. Each tithi has a classical suitability — e.g. Ekadashi
  for vrata, Purnima for full-moon observances.
- **Vaar** — the weekday, ruled by the day's planetary lord (Sun for Ravivara,
  Moon for Somavara, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn in order). Each vaar
  carries its own Rahu Kaal segment ([city panchang](/panchang)).
- **Nakshatra** — the Moon's current lunar mansion, with start and end instants.
- **Yoga** — (Sun longitude + Moon longitude) ÷ 13°20′ gives 27 yogas from
  Vishkambha to Vaidhriti. Their nature ranges from auspicious (Siddhi, Shubha,
  Siddha) to testing (Vyatipata, Vaidhriti, Parigha, Shula).
- **Karana** — half of a tithi; sixty karanas per month built from eleven names —
  seven movable (Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitila, Gara, Vanija, Vishti) cycling eight
  times, plus four fixed ones at the boundaries. Vishti (Bhadra) is avoided for
  auspicious beginnings.

## Inauspicious day-segments

Within the daylight portion of each vaar, classical proportional tables give the
windows of **Rahu Kaal**, **Yamaganda** and **Gulika** — segments where new
beginnings are traditionally avoided. Vyotish computes these to the minute for your
city, flagged for Jyotishi review pending final sign-off.

## Auspicious windows

The **Abhijit muhurta** (the middle eighth of the day, centred on solar noon) and
**Brahma muhurta** (96→48 minutes before sunrise) are universally favourable windows
on every [panchang page](/panchang). For a chosen activity, the
[muhurat finder](/muhurat) scores candidate windows across all limbs.

## Hindu month and Samvat

Panchang also names the lunar month — Amanta and Purnimanta systems — with the paksha,
adhika (leap month) flag, and the running **Vikram** and **Shaka** Samvat years,
all computed live in the engine.
