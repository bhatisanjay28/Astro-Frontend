The zodiac divides into **27 nakshatras** (lunar mansions), each exactly **13°20′** wide.
Where the rashis describe broad temperament, the nakshatra of the Moon at birth —
the **janma nakshatra** — is the finer identity layer that drives Vimshottari dasha,
matching and many muhurat rules. Each nakshatra has four **padas** (quarters of 3°20′).

The lord cycle repeats a fixed order — Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter,
Saturn, Mercury — which is exactly the **Vimshottari dasha** sequence and its year
lengths (7, 20, 6, 10, 7, 18, 16, 19, 17 years).

## Why nakshatras matter

- **Dasha**: the balance of dasha at birth is computed from how far the Moon had
  travelled through its nakshatra — see your own timeline on the
  [kundli page](/kundli).
- **Matching**: Gana (temperament), Yoni and Nadi — three of the eight
  [Ashtakoota kootas](/matching) — are read purely from the two janma nakshatras.
- **Daily panchang**: the Moon's nakshatra window appears on every
  [city panchang](/panchang) with its exact start and end instants.
